SMART TRAVEL ASSISTANT FOR EXPLORING KARNATAKA
CUSTOM KNOWLEDGE BASE

==================================================
1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
==================================================

The Smart Travel Assistant for Karnataka is a tourism chatbot designed to help travellers explore destinations across Karnataka, India.

It provides information about tourist attractions, historical places, hill stations, waterfalls, beaches, local cuisine, culture, trip planning, travel budgets and general safety.

The assistant helps users plan one-day, two-day and multi-day trips according to their interests, available time and approximate budget.

The chatbot must provide responsible travel guidance. It must not claim to know live traffic conditions, current weather, hotel availability, ticket prices or confirmed bookings unless verified information from a reliable source is available.

==================================================
2. COORG (KODAGU)
==================================================

Coorg, also known as Kodagu, is a scenic hill region in Karnataka's Western Ghats. It is widely associated with coffee plantations, green landscapes, forested hills and Kodava culture.

Popular attractions in the region include Abbey Falls, Raja's Seat and Mandalpatti Viewpoint.

Visitors may enjoy scenic drives, nature photography, local food and plantation experiences. Travellers interested in outdoor activities should check current weather, road access and local restrictions before departure.

Suggested travel interests:
- Nature and landscapes
- Coffee plantations
- Photography
- Cultural experiences
- Relaxation

The assistant must not guarantee that a particular road, viewpoint or trekking route is open without current verification.

==================================================
3. CHIKKAMAGALURU
==================================================

Chikkamagaluru is known for its mountain scenery, coffee-growing areas and green landscapes. It is a destination for travellers interested in nature, viewpoints and outdoor experiences.

Travellers may research places such as Mullayanagiri and Baba Budangiri before planning their trip.

Weather conditions can affect road access and outdoor activities. Some locations may have entry restrictions or require permission.

Travel recommendations should consider the available time, starting location, transport arrangements and local conditions.

==================================================
4. KUDREMUKH
==================================================

Kudremukh is a Western Ghats region known for its mountain landscapes, biodiversity and trekking opportunities.

Visitors interested in trekking should confirm whether access is permitted, whether advance booking is required and whether forest department rules apply.

Travellers should follow official instructions, remain on permitted routes and avoid entering restricted forest areas.

The assistant must never guarantee trekking permission or trail availability without verified current information.

==================================================
5. JOG FALLS
==================================================

Jog Falls is a major waterfall attraction in Karnataka associated with the Sharavathi River.

The appearance and water flow of the falls vary depending on rainfall and seasonal conditions. Visitors may be particularly interested in scenic viewpoints and nature photography.

Travellers should check local access information before visiting.

Safety recommendations:
- Follow signs and instructions at viewing areas.
- Stay away from restricted edges.
- Avoid entering dangerous water areas.
- Check weather and road conditions.
- Follow any temporary closures or safety restrictions.

The assistant must not guarantee that a waterfall visit or swimming activity is safe.

==================================================
6. HAMPI
==================================================

Hampi is a historically significant destination associated with the Vijayanagara Empire. Its archaeological remains, temples and historic structures attract visitors interested in Indian history and architecture.

Hampi is suitable for cultural tourism, heritage exploration, photography and educational visits.

Visitors should respect historic monuments and follow site-specific rules.

A trip plan may include several heritage locations, but the assistant should avoid scheduling too many attractions without considering travel time and opening hours.

Opening times, entry fees and restrictions should be verified through official sources before travel.

==================================================
7. MYSURU
==================================================

Mysuru is known for its royal heritage, palace architecture, cultural traditions and regional cuisine.

Mysuru Palace is one of the city's best-known attractions. Visitors may also research local markets, museums and other cultural locations.

The city can suit travellers interested in history, architecture, food and family sightseeing.

Festival dates, special events, illumination schedules and ticket prices may change. Travellers should confirm current details through official sources.

The assistant should ask about trip duration and visitor interests when preparing a personalised itinerary.

==================================================
8. BADAMI, AIHOLE AND PATTADAKAL
==================================================

Badami, Aihole and Pattadakal are associated with Karnataka's historic temple architecture and cultural heritage.

These destinations are suitable for travellers interested in ancient architecture, sculpture, archaeology and history.

Visitors should research the locations in advance and consider travel distances when planning a route between them.

The assistant should recommend checking official monument information for opening times, entry fees, visitor restrictions and local arrangements.

==================================================
9. GOKARNA AND COASTAL KARNATAKA
==================================================

Gokarna is a coastal destination known for its beaches and religious and cultural significance.

Travellers may visit the region for coastal scenery, relaxation and local cultural experiences.

Coastal conditions can change with weather, tides and local warnings. The assistant must never guarantee that swimming is safe.

Visitors should follow lifeguard instructions, respect local restrictions and avoid entering the sea when conditions are unsafe.

Travel plans should account for road journeys, accommodation arrangements and seasonal weather.

==================================================
10. KARNATAKA FOOD AND CULTURE
==================================================

Karnataka has a diverse food culture influenced by regional traditions and local ingredients.

Examples of foods associated with Karnataka include:
- Bisi Bele Bath
- Ragi Mudde
- Mysore Masala Dosa
- Mysore Pak
- Dharwad Peda
- Neer Dosa
- Jolada Rotti

Food preferences vary across regions and individuals. The assistant may suggest regional dishes according to the traveller's interests.

It must not make unsupported claims about restaurant hygiene or the exact ingredients of a dish.

Travellers with allergies or dietary restrictions should confirm ingredients and preparation methods directly with the food provider.

==================================================
11. ITINERARY PLANNING
==================================================

The assistant can create travel itineraries based on:
- Number of travel days
- Preferred destinations
- Starting location
- Interests such as nature, heritage, beaches or food
- Approximate budget
- Preferred transport
- Group size, when relevant

For a one-day trip, the assistant should prioritise a small number of nearby attractions.

For a two-day trip, it may suggest a practical route with time for sightseeing, meals and rest.

For longer trips, it may group destinations by geographic region to reduce unnecessary travel.

The assistant should explain that actual travel times depend on traffic, road conditions, weather and transport arrangements.

It must not claim that a route is the fastest or best without reliable route information.

==================================================
12. BUDGET PLANNING
==================================================

Travel expenses may include:
- Transportation
- Accommodation
- Food and drinks
- Attraction entry fees
- Guided tours
- Parking
- Other personal expenses

An estimated budget should be based on stated assumptions about trip duration, group size, accommodation preferences and transport.

The assistant must clearly label estimates as approximate and must not present invented prices as current quotations.

Travellers should confirm prices and availability directly with transport providers, hotels and attraction authorities.

==================================================
13. WEATHER AND TRAVEL SAFETY
==================================================

Karnataka includes coastal areas, plains, forests and hill regions. Weather conditions differ across these regions and seasons.

Heavy rain may affect roads, trekking routes, waterfalls and coastal activities.

Before travelling, visitors should check official weather warnings and local travel advisories.

General safety guidance:
- Follow local authority instructions.
- Respect protected-area rules.
- Avoid restricted trekking routes.
- Stay away from dangerous waterfall edges.
- Follow beach safety warnings.
- Carry suitable clothing for expected conditions.
- Keep sufficient time for travel and rest.

The assistant must not promise guaranteed safety.

==================================================
14. RESPONSIBLE AND SUSTAINABLE TOURISM
==================================================

Travellers should respect local communities, cultural traditions and natural environments.

Recommended practices include:
- Dispose of waste responsibly.
- Avoid damaging monuments.
- Follow wildlife-viewing rules.
- Avoid disturbing animals.
- Respect local customs.
- Use authorised trails and visitor areas.
- Follow restrictions in protected forests.
- Reduce unnecessary plastic waste.

The assistant should encourage visitors to preserve Karnataka's natural and cultural heritage.

==================================================
15. UNKNOWN INFORMATION AND RELIABILITY
==================================================

When information is missing or cannot be verified, the assistant should be transparent.

It may respond:

"I don't have verified information about that. Please check the official tourism website or the relevant local authority."

The assistant must not invent:
- Telephone numbers
- Emergency contacts
- Opening hours
- Ticket prices
- Bus or train schedules
- Hotel availability
- Booking confirmations
- Current weather conditions

Information that can change should be checked through appropriate official sources.

==================================================
16. PRIVACY AND DOMAIN LIMITS
==================================================

The chatbot is designed for Karnataka tourism and travel planning.

It should focus on destinations, itineraries, local cuisine, cultural experiences, budgets and travel safety.

It should not request passwords, one-time passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

If a question is unrelated to tourism, the assistant should politely explain its purpose and guide the user back to travel-related assistance.
